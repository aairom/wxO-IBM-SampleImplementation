# IBM Bob Bobcoins — Usage and Budget

Source: https://bob.ibm.com/docs/ide/account/bobcoins

## What are Bobcoins?

Bobcoins are IBM Bob's usage metric that convert token usage into a **predictable budget system**. One Bobcoin represents a standardized amount of computational resources, including token usage across different models and operations.

You don't need to track tokens directly—IBM Bob automatically converts your model usage into Bobcoins. Different models and operations consume tokens at different rates, but Bobcoins provide a consistent way to understand and manage your usage.

## Bobcoins vs. Tokens

- **Tokens** — Raw units of model usage representing pieces of text (words, characters) that the AI model processes. Every request consumes input tokens (your prompt and context) and output tokens (Bob's response).
- **Bobcoins** — IBM Bob's usage metric. Converts token usage into a predictable budget system.

## Monthly Budget

Your Bobcoin budget determines how much you can use IBM Bob each month.

- **Budget resets** on a specific day each month (check the Settings panel to see your reset date).
- **Unused Bobcoins do not carry forward** to the next month.
- Your balance resets to your monthly allocated amount (based on your tier).

## Budget Tier Progression

You can permanently increase your monthly Bobcoin allocation by providing feedback:

| Tier | Monthly Bobcoins | How to reach |
|------|-----------------|--------------|
| Tier 1 | 500 | Default starting tier |
| Tier 2 | 750 | Submit the first feedback |
| Tier 3 | 1000 | Submit the second feedback |
| Tier 4 | 2000 | Submit the third feedback |

**Example:** If you submit feedback twice and reach tier 3, you will start every month with 1000 Bobcoins.

**Note:** If you have less than 500 Bobcoins, you must use the feedback system when prompted to get updated to the tier 1 amount.

## Monitoring Your Usage

You can track your Bobcoin usage in two ways:

1. **Budget gauge** — Hover your cursor over the gauge component in the top-right corner of the Bob panel to see your remaining balance.
2. **Chat interface** — When a conversation is open, view your token and Bobcoin usage in the top-right corner of the chat interface.

## Managing Your Budget Effectively

- **Provide feedback early** — Submit feedback when prompted to increase your budget tier before running out.
- **Monitor your usage regularly** — Keep track of your Bobcoin balance to plan your work.
- **Use only required context** — Start new chats when the task changes to avoid carrying over irrelevant context.
- **Limit MCP server use** — Excessive use of MCP servers can significantly increase Bobcoin consumption.
- **Share meaningful feedback** — Quality feedback helps improve IBM Bob and increases your budget.

## Enterprise Bobcoins

On the Enterprise plan, Bobcoins are managed through a **shared pool**:

- Distribute Bobcoins from a centralized budget to empower your entire team.
- Admins can set individual spending limits.
- Purchase additional Bobcoin packs as your team grows.
- Monitor Bobcoin consumption through the admin dashboard.
