# IBM Bob Bobalytics — Analytics Dashboard

Source: https://bob.ibm.com/docs/ide/features/bobalytics

## What is Bobalytics?

Bobalytics provides **analytics and insights** into how IBM Bob contributes to your software development process. Use it to:

- Understand Bob's impact on your codebase.
- Track Bobcoin spending.
- Monitor team adoption across your organization.

**Note:** Bobalytics is available **exclusively for Enterprise plan users**.

## Accessing Bobalytics

1. Go to [bob.ibm.com](https://bob.ibm.com) and log in.
2. Click **Admin**, then select the **Bobalytics** tab.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Bobalytics is organized around three KPIs:

### 1. Adoption Rate

Adoption rate measures how consistently your team uses Bob. It is calculated as the average number of daily active users divided by the total number of licensed seats. A user is counted as active on a given day when they accept at least one code completion or complete a chat conversation.

**Primary metrics:**
- Team onboarding: adoption percentage for specific teams.
- Daily active users: a chart of active users over time.
- Team details and trends: Adoption, value, and cost metrics.

**Supporting metrics:**
- Usage levels: breakdown of users by how often they use Bob (multiple times per day, daily, weekly, occasional, or inactive).
- Bob's repository impact: repositories where Bob users have been most active.

**Tip:** If adoption rate is low, check usage levels to identify inactive or occasional users.

### 2. Bob Factor

Measures the value Bob brings to your software development process.

### 3. Bobcoin Spend

Tracks Bobcoin consumption across your organization.

## Using Bobalytics Effectively

Check Bobalytics on a regular cadence — weekly or monthly — to spot trends:

- A **declining adoption rate** over several weeks may signal that users need additional enablement.
- A **sudden spike in Bobcoin spend** can be investigated by filtering down to a specific team or time range.
- A **steadily rising Bob factor** alongside stable spend is a strong signal that your team is getting increasing value from Bob.

## Telemetry Requirement

Enterprise plan users must keep telemetry enabled to view data in Bobalytics. Disabling telemetry will prevent your usage data from appearing in analytics dashboards.
